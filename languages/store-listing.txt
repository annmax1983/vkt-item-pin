<<<<<<<<<< ENGLISH · SHORT >>>>>>>>>>

Pin products from any shopping site: title, price, link and model saved locally with price history. Free 50, Pro unlimited.

<<<<<<<<<< ENGLISH · DETAILED >>>>>>>>>>

VKT Item Pin is a shopping research extension that captures product snapshots with one click: title, price, link, model, site and date are saved to a local library — with automatic price-history tracking.

Comparing products across shopping sites usually means dozens of open tabs and copy-pasting into spreadsheets. Instead, just hover any product, click the 📌 Pin button, and keep a growing price-tracked library right in your browser.

🎯 HOVER TO CAPTURE
Hover a product on a product page or in a search-result grid, click Pin — title, price, link, model, site and date are extracted automatically. A preview panel lets you edit fields before saving, and "Pick" mode lets you click any element on the page to fill a missing field.

📈 PRICE HISTORY + TARGET ALERTS
Revisit a product you already pinned and the price is recorded for you — one point per day, no second click. Set a target price per item: the first visit at or below it shows a desktop notification, entirely local, with no server and no email. Tracked products never count against your free quota.

🛒 24 SITE PRESETS + UNIVERSAL ENGINE
Hand-tuned selectors cover 24 popular marketplaces worldwide, from global giants to regional favorites. Every other shop (Shopify, WooCommerce, independent stores) is covered by a universal engine: JSON-LD structured data → Open Graph tags → generic selectors → manual pick mode.

🗂 DASHBOARD
Search and organize your library by date or site, edit records, correct or remove bad price points, and view trend charts. Add a note and up to 10 tags per item — both searchable — and read a summary strip with the price drops of the last 30 days plus the total value of a single-currency library. One click jumps to Google Shopping, eBay or Amazon to check the same title elsewhere. Compare (Pro) overlays the price history of up to 6 products in one chart.

💾 YOUR DATA, YOUR BROWSER
Everything is stored locally via chrome.storage.local. Nothing is uploaded, no tracking, no analytics. Export/import a full JSON backup (notes, tags and target prices included), or export CSV (Pro) for spreadsheets. A storage monitor warns you before local storage gets full.

🆓 FREE PLAN
50 products, automatic price tracking on every revisit, target-price alerts, notes and tags, JSON backup, storage monitor, 6 languages.

⭐ PRO
Unlimited products, advanced filters (site / tag / date range / price range), CSV export with notes and target prices, multi-item compare (up to 6 products in one chart).

🔒 PRIVACY FIRST
All extraction and storage happen locally in your browser. The only network request is optional license validation: activation, plus a periodic re-check (at most once every 2 hours) while a Pro license is active. No webpage content is ever read or uploaded.

🌍 English, 中文, 日本語, Español, Deutsch, Français.

Works on Chrome, Edge and other Chromium-based browsers. Manifest V3, minimal permissions.
